About

002

Rashmika is a BSc Honours Chemistry graduate turned commercial analyst, with end-to-end FMCG experience at AB InBev/SAB spanning food safety, quality, finance and digital marketplace analytics. She began in the lab — six months at the University of KwaZulu-Natal synthesising antitubercular and antimalarial drug candidates, work that resulted in two peer-reviewed papers.

That same discipline now shows up in her reporting work — automating manual processes and leading a brewery's first BRC/GFSI food-safety certification. She's currently a Tech Sales Analyst on the Digital Marketplace team, owning financial, sales-insights and partner-portfolio reporting across AB InBev Marketplace ZA's four divisions, where a dashboard has to be right the first time, every month.
